What the preview provides

The v0.2.0 package includes workflow instructions, an authenticated MCP connector, an Odoo 19 addon and an interactive dashboard. The website’s illustrative mockup and sample outputs use fictional data; they are not a connection to your database.

Live operation requires a separately configured deployment, addon installation, suitable API availability, authentication and user permissions. Other Odoo versions and individual custom modules need compatibility validation. This developer preview has not been represented as a production-validated service.

Your responsibilities

  • Use only Odoo accounts, instances and records you are authorized to access.
  • Follow your organization’s confidentiality, security and data-protection requirements.
  • Enter API keys only in the deployed connector’s secure setup form, never in chat or public support issues.
  • Review the exact preview, company, acting user and side effects before confirming a write.
  • Validate the addon and your custom mappings on staging before production writes.
  • Do not use a different identity or elevated credentials to bypass denied access.

Odoo remains the system of record. Assistant recommendations and calculations need your review. Verification checks a saved result; it does not prove that the underlying real-world work was completed.

Deployment and third-party services

Your operator is responsible for hosting, OAuth configuration, key management, backups, retention, access control and operational monitoring. Custom Odoo automation may cause notifications or external actions that cannot be undone by a database rollback.

OpenAI, Odoo, your hosting/identity providers and GitHub supply separate services governed by their own agreements and policies. Downloading this preview does not provide hosting, an Odoo subscription or third-party accounts.
